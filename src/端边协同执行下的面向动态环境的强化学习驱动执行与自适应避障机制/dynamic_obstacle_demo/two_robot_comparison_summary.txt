Two-robot avoidance metric comparison
强化学习避障 is compared with 贪心反应式APF基线.
Baseline definition: greedy artificial potential field using current obstacle positions only; no lookahead prediction, no corridor priority yielding, weak robot-to-robot repulsion, oscillatory local-minimum escape, reduced cruising speed, and no final hard robot separation.

Metrics:
- success_rate: fraction of robots that reach goals.
- mean_arrival_time_s: lower is better.
- global_min_clearance_m: higher is safer; negative means collision or overlap.
- collision_or_overlap_samples: lower is safer.
- mean_abs_accel_mps2: lower is smoother.
